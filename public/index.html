How to find the URL

Follow along on bookmyshow.com. Four small clicks and you're done.

  1. 1

    Open your movie's page

    Pick your city on BookMyShow, then open the movie you want to watch. Scroll to the big red Book tickets button and click it.

  2. 2

    Pick language & format

    A popup asks for language and format. Click one — usually English → 2D, unless you want IMAX / 4DX / Dolby.

  3. 3

    Choose a theater & showtime

    You'll see a list of theaters with all showtimes. Pick the date at the top, find your theater in the list, and click the showtime button you're interested in (e.g. 06:55 PM).

  4. 4

    Copy the URL

    You'll land on the seat-map page. Click on your browser's address bar, select the whole URL (Cmd/Ctrl + A), copy it (Cmd/Ctrl + C), and paste it into Seat Watcher. The URL will look like:

    https://in.bookmyshow.com/movies/hyd/seat-layout/ET…/…/…/YYYYMMDD

Common questions

My city isn't Hyderabad or Visakhapatnam. Only those two are supported today. Ask the dev to add yours.

My URL keeps being rejected as "past-dated." The showtime you picked has already started or ended. Pick a future one.

The card says "sold canvas not rendered." BookMyShow is being slow. It should self-heal on the next poll.
